## What's new in 0.3.8

### Inbox: setlists and Scripture by email
WorshipUI can now have its own email address. Worship leaders, pastors and readers email their setlist or Scripture reading to it. The emails appear under **✉ Inbox** next to the church name at the top, with a number showing how many are new.

**Set it up once (on the church computer)**

1. Pick an email address for WorshipUI. A separate account just for this is best, for example *worship@yourchurch.org* or a new Gmail account such as *gracechurch.worship@gmail.com*. WorshipUI can read every email in that inbox, so don't use a personal one.
2. In WorshipUI, open **Settings → Inbox** and type the address and its password. Then choose **Connect inbox**. WorshipUI finds the mail server by itself.
   - **Gmail:** Gmail won't accept the normal password from apps. In the Google account, turn on 2-Step Verification, then create an *app password* at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and paste that instead.
   - **Church or website email** (such as info@yourchurch.org): the normal password usually works. If WorshipUI can't find the server, enter it under **More options**. Your web host can tell you what it is.
3. Share the address with your team.

**What people send**

- **A setlist:** put *Setlist* or *Songs* in the subject and one song per line. Numbers and bullets are fine.
- **Scripture:** put *Scripture* or *Reading* in the subject and the reference, such as *John 3:16-18*. Spanish works too (*Juan 3:16*). They can paste the verse text under the reference, or leave it out and WorshipUI will use the Bible on the computer.
- **Anything else** shows up as a message to read.

**Using it**

- Choose **✉ Inbox**. Each email shows who sent it and what WorshipUI found in it.
- **Setlists:** tick the songs you want, then choose **Add songs to the service**. Songs already in your song library come in with their lyrics and style. New songs are added with just their title, so open them and add the lyrics.
- **Scripture:** choose **Add to the service** to add the passage as slides.
- **Dismiss** (or **Done**) clears an email from the list. Nothing is added unless you choose it.
- WorshipUI checks for new mail every 3 minutes and when you open the Inbox. It only reads the inbox: it never deletes emails, marks them as read or sends anything.
- The password is kept on that computer only, encrypted by Windows or macOS.
